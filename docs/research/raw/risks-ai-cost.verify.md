## Summary

The report mostly holds. Prices, terms and Android policy quotes reproduce against the primary pages. The two findings that matter most:

1. **Play service fees changed on 2026-06-30 in the US, UK and EEA (France included).** One-time purchases in the first $1M are now **20% + 5% billing fee (new installs)** or **25% + 5% (existing installs)**, not 15%. Subscriptions are **10% + 5% = 15%**. This contradicts §5 ("15% on the first $1M") and weakens the one-time-unlock options (B) and (C). [verified: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622]
2. **The #1 reminder-reliability mitigation rests on an interaction neither page documents.** The power-details page caps the Restricted bucket at "One alarm per day, either an exact alarm or an inexact alarm" and says nothing about `setAlarmClock()`. The alarms page says the system "never adjusts" setAlarmClock delivery time and "leaves low-power modes". That sentence is about Doze and time adjustment, not bucket quotas. Whether setAlarmClock escapes the Restricted-bucket cap is **could-not-check**.

## Deprecations / changes found
- MediaPipe LLM Inference API is maintenance-only on Android, iOS and Web; use LiteRT-LM.
- Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite shuts down 2027-05-07 (replacement gemini-3.5-flash-lite). Build on 3.5 Flash-Lite or 3.8 Flash.
- Gemini 3.8 Flash prices double on 2027-01-01.
- Google Play's fee structure changed on 2026-06-30 for the US, UK and EEA (a 5% billing fee is split out; one-time purchases are 20 to 25% + 5%).
- chrono_dart is effectively unmaintained (about 2 years) and English only.
- Claude 4.7+ tokenizer makes about 30% more tokens per text, which affects cross-provider cost comparisons.
- Android 17 adds a `setExactAndAllowWhileIdle(OnAlarmListener)` overload. Apps targeting 17 face background-audio hardening: a foreground service needs while-in-use capabilities unless the app holds an exact-alarm permission and uses USAGE_ALARM audio.

## Missed angles
- The Claude tokenizer inflation (about 30%) is not in the cost model.
- Android 17 behaviour changes (background audio hardening for a sounding reminder, the new alarm-listener overload) are not covered. Only 12, 13, 14 and 16 are discussed.
- Whether setAlarmClock is exempt from the Restricted-bucket cap needs an on-device test (adb `am set-standby-bucket <pkg> restricted`, then schedule several setAlarmClock alarms).
- The revoke-kills-process behaviour of SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, and backup-restore denying it on Android 14, are not mentioned.
- The new Play fee structure in the EEA changes the one-time-unlock economics. Web or external-link billing (20%) may be an option.
- The free-tier Gemini ban applies to EEA/CH/UK **users**, so even a personal build used by the developer in France must use paid Gemini. Of the providers covered, only Anthropic and Gemini-paid were checked for training terms; **OpenAI API data-use terms remain unchecked**.
- Not re-verified in this pass (the report's grades carry over, unconfirmed): competitor prices, Apple $99/yr, Flathub, Snap, Gemini Nano/AICore beta status, Apple Foundation Models context size, F-Droid statements, Microsoft publisher verification and the Google OAuth testing-mode 100-user cap / 7-day token figures.
