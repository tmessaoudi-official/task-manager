## FR/EN
- FR: "tenu" [tə.ny] = kept/held ("promesse tenue", "être tenu de" = to be bound to). Homophone of "tenue" = outfit/attire/bearing, which is harmless. Nothing embarrassing.
- EN: no meaning. It will be said "TEN-oo", not the French [y]. That is easy to spell, but the pronunciation differs between the languages.
- Evocation: the commitment-kept meaning is clear only to French speakers. For English speakers it does not signal tasks/todos, which is the same weakness that got "Holdfast" rejected. There is also a visual and aural risk of confusion with "Temu".
