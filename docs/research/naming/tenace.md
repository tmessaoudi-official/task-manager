## French and English
- FR "tenace" /tə.nas/: tenacious, stubborn, persistent. Positive, with a light "hard to get rid of" sense (une tache tenace, a stubborn stain). Nothing embarrassing.
- EN: "tenace" is a real but obscure word, a bridge/whist term for a card combination such as A-Q. English speakers will often say it "TEN-ace" or "ten-AYSS", which is what lets 10Ace ("ten ace") use tenace.app. They will spell it from "tenacious", so it is spellable. The pronunciation is not the same in the two languages.
- It suggests persistence (nag until done) but not tasks or reminders on its own, which is the same weakness the owner saw in "Holdfast".
